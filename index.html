About SafeShield

Gender-based violence and crime affect families and neighbourhoods across South Africa. SafeShield exists so that anyone can reach help quickly, share their location with people they trust, and learn how to stay safe.

Our mission

End GBV and crime and build a safe community, with tools that are simple to use under stress.

What we are not

We are not the police or a medical service. SafeShield connects you to those services and to your own support network faster.

    If you are in danger

    • Call 10111 or the GBV Command Centre on 0800 428 428.
    • Move toward people, light and open businesses.
    • Tell someone your location and keep your phone charged.
    • After a sexual assault, seek medical care as soon as possible. Treatment to prevent HIV works best within 72 hours.
    • Keep clothing and messages as evidence if you can.
